One sentence, up to 200 characters, about the best advice ever received — an entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Best Advice I Ever Got"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pangg7-the-best-advice-i-ever-got-358cefb0).

## The entry

`main.py` writes a single sentence to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`) and prints the same sentence to stdout. The sentence recalls a real moment — a father wiping down the family food stall past midnight — and the advice he gave there: don't chase the money; feed the people who keep coming back, and it will find you.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
